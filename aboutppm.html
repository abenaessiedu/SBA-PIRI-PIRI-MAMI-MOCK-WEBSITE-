Meet the Original PiriPiriMami™️

Abena Essiedu is a interdisciplinary creative based in New York City.

A graduate of NYU's Tisch School of the Arts, she believes in creating culture shifting media for the Gen-Z generation. PiriPiriMami is a platform dedicated to being acurated comprehensive living resource focusing primarily on the food—culture—and holistic wellness.

WELCOME TO THE WORLD OF PIRI PIRI MAMI

THE WORLD OF PIRI PIRI MAMI is centered around storytelling, and nourishment. This is an online platform that features a Quarterly Magazine Issue centered around the Seasons of Our Lives.
